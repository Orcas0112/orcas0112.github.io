# Twilight Bazaar — Variant Era roadmap (after V9.1)

V9.1 Shiny Surge + Fusion Reborn introduces 0.9% independent Shiny rolls on normal pack pulls, a Shiny Forge, and upgraded high-rarity fusion with protected original Mysticals.

## V9.2 — Shadow & Glitch

Introduce additional cosmetic variants to the existing Blooks, distinct from rarity and without inventing new characters. Variant shelves, filters, previews and reliable save/import support.

## V9.3 — Prismatic Vault

Extremely rare Prismatic variants with transparent pack odds, advanced variant search and rare-pull reveal polish. Avoid unverified copyrighted artwork and avoid unrealistic claims about official odds.

## V9.4 — Variant Trading & Showcases

Trading rules that protect first/last copies by default; separate regular versus variant inventory, favorites and showcases for the user's rarest originals. Clear inventory confirmations and trade history.

## V9.5 — Ascension & Mystical Prestige

Optional long-term variant challenges using duplicates and shards; earn progression cosmetics and showcase effects rather than sacrificing the five original Mysticals. Maintain existing Event Hall trials.

## Permanent rules

No custom packs or new AI Blook artwork unless separately approved. Retain the current regular and archival packs; keep original published pack odds, 50 virtual tokens per pack, admin local-only access, and save compatibility. No real-money betting, buying, cash-out or prizes. Treat public GitHub Pages passwords as casual local gates, not real security.
