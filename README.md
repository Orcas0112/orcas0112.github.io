# Twilight Bazaar V9.0.4 — Every Blook Obtainable

Independent, browser-local virtual-token simulator, not affiliated with Blooket.

## What's new

- **34 openable pack categories:** original-themed packs remain at their previous snapshot rates, the six former starter galleries are now actual 50-token packs, and eight new simulator-only archive packs provide random-pull routes for legacy Blooks.
- **Rare Finds, Ultra Finds, Secret Finds, Space Color Vault, Seasonal Vault, History Pack, Contest Showcase Pack and Game Mode Pack.** These use **Twilight Bazaar-only probabilities**; none claim to be official Blooket market packs or probabilities. No new custom characters or artwork were created.
- All **386 non-Mystical catalog entries** have a pack row with a positive draw rate. The five original Mysticals remain available through their demanding Event Hall trials (and cannot be pulled from regular packs or fusion).
- **No new independent Rare/Ultra/Secret bonus finds:** they now live inside named packs that you choose to open. Old bonus history stays readable in previous openings.
- **Fusion odds booster:** fuse any two owned Blooks and optionally sacrifice up to **three** extra Blooks, from any rarity, to increase the chances of high-rarity rewards. The game displays five recalculated probabilities before confirmation. All selected Blooks are consumed for one prize; no Mysticals in the fusion reward pool.
- Last-copy protection on by default; protected originals require an explicit override and confirmation. Previous V9 fusions and local saves remain compatible.
- Admin panel accepts `Orcas0112` (not secure server-side authentication; affects only local storage).
- Image Checker from V9.0.3 retained. Rare historical/game-mode variants may still have unavailable remote artwork; no placeholder characters or AI art have been substituted.

## Installation

1. **Back up:** Export your save from Vault & Settings in the existing website.
2. Extract this ZIP locally. Upload the **individual files** into the root of `orcas0112/orcas0112.github.io`, branch `main`, replacing matching filenames, then commit.
3. Keep GitHub Pages publishing from `main / (root)`; visit https://orcas0112.github.io/ and hard-refresh (`Command + Shift + R` on Mac).
4. Confirm header **V9.0.4**. Pack Wall shows starter and archive groups. Fusion Lab offers three extra-ingredient slots.

**Save key:** `twilightBazaarWebSaveV1`. Uploading a new release does not reset same-origin local storage. Legacy custom-pack Blooks removed previously do not reappear. Old unused `tb83-*.svg` files may remain on GitHub if you uploaded them before; this release does not link to them.

**Distinguish official vs simulator:** Some entries represent unreleased archival names, contest showcases, or game-mode appearances rather than actual official Market collectibles. They are obtainable only *within this fan simulator*, and their archive-pack rates are arbitrary game design choices, not verified Blooket odds.
